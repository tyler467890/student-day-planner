Important on iPhone & iPad

Add Calo to your Home Screen first, then enter your code

On iPhone and iPad, the Home Screen app and Safari count as two separate devices. Add it first so your code is only used once.

  1. Open the link in Safari. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen. If you see Open as Web App, turn it on. Tap Add.
  3. Open Calo from your Home Screen. Calo says hi: type your code and tap Unlock.

Android phone or tablet

In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). Open Calo from your home screen and enter your code.

Laptop or computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar (or the menu, then Install Calo). Or just bookmark it. Then enter your code.

Calo Good to know

A few friendly tips

Keep this page handy. It answers the most common questions.

Your code

One code, up to 3 of your devices

Your code works on up to 3 of your devices, for example your phone, tablet and laptop. Please don't share it. It's just for you.

Got a new phone, or Calo says your code is already on 3 devices? Message me on Etsy and I'll reset your code so you can use it again.

Your plans

Saved on your device, private to you

There's no account. Your plans stay on the device you use, so they don't sync between your phone and laptop.

Back up every so often: in Calo, open Customize and tap Back up now. It saves a file you can bring back later with Restore from backup. Clearing your browser's website data can erase your plans, so a backup keeps them safe.

Offline

Works without internet

You need internet once to unlock. After that, Calo keeps working offline.

Reminders

Best while Calo is open

Reminders show while Calo is open. They may not arrive when it's fully closed, so don't rely on them for anything urgent.
