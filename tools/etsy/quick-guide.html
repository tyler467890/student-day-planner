Calo Quick guide

How Calo works

Points, coins, levels and streaks on one page.

Points

Every finished task counts

Easy+5
Medium+10
Hard+20
Day done+10

You get the Day done bonus when at least two tasks are finished.

Coins & Pet Shop

Dress up your pet

Every 5 points = 1 coin. Tap your pet, then Shop, to buy outfits and accessories. Trying things on is always free, and nothing costs real money.

Levels

Level up as you go

Level 2 starts at 40 points. Level 10 starts at 1,180 points. Your pet celebrates every new level.

Streaks

Keep your streak going

Finish one task to count the day. Each day starts at 4:00 AM, so late-night work still counts. You get one rest day a week that won't break your streak.

Make it yours

Themes and pets

Open Customize to pick one of 6 colour themes, your own colours, or a photo background. Spin your pet with your finger, and Calo pops in with pep talks.

Reminders

Reminders work while Calo is open

Give a task a time and a reminder. Allow notifications when asked. Reminders show while Calo is open, but may not arrive when it's fully closed, so don't count on them for anything urgent.

!

Back up your plans

Plans live on your device and don't sync. In Customize, tap Back up now to save a file, and use Restore from backup to bring it back on any device.
